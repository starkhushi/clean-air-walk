🫁 If you feel unwell outside in bad air (English / हिंदी)
  • Stop and slow down. Harder breathing pulls in more polluted air. Walk instead of running.
  • Move indoors or into a closed car or building, away from busy roads.
  • If you have asthma, use your reliever inhaler as your doctor has told you, and follow your asthma action plan.
  • Drink water and rest until breathing feels normal.
  • Get urgent medical help (call 112) if you have severe breathlessness, chest pain or tightness, blue or grey lips, confusion, or an inhaler that isn't helping.
  • Next time: pick a Good or Satisfactory window, go shorter or lighter, and consider a well-fitted N95 mask. Cloth and loose surgical masks do little against PM2.5.
